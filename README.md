# ✨ Dress Your Dolly — Royal Castle Studio 👑

> 💖 **Created & designed by a 7-year-old royal fashion designer and her dad!**  
> A magical, super-fun 2D dress-up game filled with ballgowns, sparkles, pets, and royal runway celebrations.

---

## 🌟 Welcome to the Castle!

**Dress Your Dolly** is a complete, self-contained web game made with pure HTML5 Canvas, CSS, JavaScript, and synthesized chime sounds — with **zero external image or audio files needed**. Just open `index.html` in any web browser and start styling right away!

---

## 🎨 Magical Features

### 🏰 3 Royal Castle Scenes
Switch between fairytale locations anytime:
- **Grand Royal Ballroom**: Shimmering marble pillars, golden crystal chandeliers, and a red carpet.
- **Enchanted Castle Garden**: Blooming rose bushes, a splashing tiered marble fountain, and fluttering colorful butterflies.
- **Starlight Balcony**: A glowing crescent moon, twinkling night stars, palace lanterns, and distant celebration fireworks!

### 👑 Character Customization (Dolly Base)
- **Skin Tones & Expressions**: 8 diverse skin tones and 7 cute expressions (Happy Smile 😊, Playful Wink 😉, Royal Poise 👑, Excited Laugh 😄, Cool Sunglasses 😎, Cute Blush 🥰, and Starry Eyes 🤩).
- **Hair Salon**: 9 hairstyles (Princess Waves, Space Buns, Braided Crown, Afro Curls, High Ponytail, and more) plus 12 colors ranging from golden blonde and chestnut to pastel pink, fairy mint, and magic purple.
- **Wardrobe & Outfits**:
  - Sparkly Ballgowns (Pink, Gold, Ice Blue, Emerald Velvet)
  - Royal Knight Armor & Superhero Bodysuits (Cosmic & Solar)
  - Starlight Wizard Robe
  - Streetwear tops & bottoms (Pastel Hoodie, Denim Jacket, Rainbow Star Tee, Pleated Skirt, Cargo Pants, Star Jeans).
- **Royal Shoes**: Glass Slippers, Sparkly Heels, High-Top Sneakers, Combat Boots, Ballet Ribbons, and Magic Floating Boots with glowing levitation rings!
- **Layerable Magic & Hero Accessories**:
  - Wear a crown, fairy wings, flowing cape, star glasses, and a magic wand all at once!
- **Cute Pet Companions**:
  - 🐉 Baby Dragon (with tiny flapping wings & sparkle breath)
  - 🦄 Fluffy Unicorn (with rainbow mane & golden horn)
  - 🐶 Royal Corgi Puppy (with a mini crown & velvet cape)
  - 🐈‍⬛ Magical Black Cat (wearing a tiny wizard hat)
  - 🔥 Baby Phoenix (with glowing embers & flame feathers)

---

## 🎉 Interactive Moments & Sound Effects
- **Web Audio API Sound Synthesizer**: Cheerful marimba pops, sparkling chime tones when equipping outfits, and a mute button.
- **🎲 Surprise Me! Button**: Instantly randomizes a fun outfit, hair color, and pet with a big shower of sparkles.
- **🔄 Reset Button**: Cleans the wardrobe back to the base dolly anytime.
- **🌟 Finish & Runway! Mode**:
  - Hides the wardrobe to spotlight Dolly on the royal carpet.
  - Plays an upbeat synthesized royal fanfare cadence.
  - Bursts into animated confetti ribbons and golden sparkles.
  - Dolly dances with a happy bounce and celebratory twirl!
  - Displays an encouraging **"Royal Fashion Judge"** 5-star rating (e.g., *"5/5 Stars! Royal Masterpiece!"*).
  - Includes a **📸 Take Photo (Download)** button to save Dolly as a high-quality PNG image to your computer.

---

## 🚀 How to Play Locally

1. Clone or download this repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/matthewpbarton/dress-your-dolly.git
   cd dress-your-dolly
   ```
2. Double-click `index.html` or open it directly in Google Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge.
3. Have fun dressing your dolly! ✨
