# ✨ Dress Your Dolly — Royal Castle Studio

A cute, vibrant, self-contained 2D "Dress Your Dolly" web game built with vanilla HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (HTML5 Canvas), and Web Audio API.

## Features
- **3 Magical Castle Scenes**: Grand Royal Ballroom, Enchanted Castle Garden, and Starlight Balcony.
- **Character Customization**:
  - 8 skin tones
  - 7 facial expressions
  - 9 hairstyles & 12 hair colors
  - 8 full outfits (sparkly ballgowns, knight armor, superhero bodysuits, wizard robe) + separate tops and bottoms
  - 7 shoe styles (including glass slippers and magic floating boots)
  - Layerable accessories across 5 sub-slots (tiaras/crowns/hats, wings, capes, eyewear/masks, wands/swords/shields)
  - 5 pet companions (Baby Dragon, Fluffy Unicorn, Royal Corgi, Magic Cat, Baby Phoenix)
- **Interactive & Audio**: Cheerful procedural synthesized chimes, fanfare melodies, and camera shutter sounds via Web Audio API.
- **Finish & Runway! Mode**: Celebratory runway spotlight, particle confetti & sparkle bursts, royal fashion judge rating banner, and a "Take Photo" PNG downloader.
- **Zero External Asset Dependencies**: 100% self-contained with no external images or audio files needed.
